## Two research hypotheses, not promoted strategies

No third hypothesis is justified by current evidence. Exact definitions below are research choices, NOT fitted estimates or implemented trading changes. No claimed ICT provenance and no AI filter.

### H1 — transient sweep response with volatility-scaled risk

Economic hypothesis: a causal reclaim/MSS signal may capture a short inventory rebound, while fixed8/2point stops remove positions before the response can develop. Reuse the frozen causal Sweep/MSS signal unchanged. At a closed signal bar, compute ATR20 as the arithmetic mean of20same-session true ranges (previous close within session; require21completed bars). Entry at next available open; stop distance=ceil(1.5*ATR20/tick)*tick, target2R; also exit at next available open after5complete minutes from entry. Do not choose1or15minutes because a retrospective markout happens to look better. Freeze distances at signal time, no trailing. Only first eligible signal/instrument/session between10:00and15:00NY, at most1contract; risk may reject it without searching for another. Continuous original daily200/global500/3loss limits unchanged. Compare to exact same signals and risk with original fixed bracket, and report changed risk exposure/occupancy rather than attributing everything to stop width.

### H2 — failed opening-auction acceptance, not breakout continuation

Economic hypothesis: a confirmed opening-range break that immediately fails may indicate unaccepted prices rather than persistent demand. Freeze09:30–09:45NYhigh/low. From10:00to11:00, require one closed bar at least1tick outside, followed within2completed bars by a close back inside. Fade at next available open only if the current ATR-independent efficiency ratio `abs(C[t]-C[t-20])/sum(abs(diff(C[t-20:t]))) <=0.25` (denominator0=>no signal). Both-boundary events are rejected. Stop1tick beyond the extreme across breakout-to-reclaim bars; target frozen opening-range midpoint, valid only if on profit side of actual raw entry; time exit at next open after15minutes. Only first qualifying event/instrument/day, max1contract, no re-entry/trailing/parameter search. Original risk remains authoritative. This is price-only failed acceptance, not proof of order-book liquidity.
