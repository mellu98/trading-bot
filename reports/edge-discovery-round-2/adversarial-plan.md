## Execution and data vetoes

1. **Reversal must survive modeled information delay.** A signal using a daily close must wait one full completed daily execution bar, then enter at the following open. The next boundary is not available merely because it shares the close timestamp. Freeze a matching finite holding/exit rule; do not claim to test an immediate bounce if the implemented lag measures a different return. H1 long-only spot is not a long-short academic replication or actual market making.
2. **Proxies are not quotes.** Quote turnover, trade count, average trade size, taker-buy share and return/volume illiquidity measures cannot establish spread, depth, queue priority, signed order-book flow or executable size. High taker-buy share is not aggregate net buying: every trade has a buyer and seller. Volume may reflect reporting/wash-trading effects. Validate any newly used raw kline fields without inspecting strategy returns, and label their limitations.
3. **Past-only transforms.** Eligibility, liquidity ranks, predictor standardization, thresholds, volatility and fitting use data available at the recorded signal cutoff. No full-sample percentiles, later membership, future-volume filters, forward returns in training, retrospective choice of signal direction or silent overlapping-label leakage. Prefix perturbation tests must preserve earlier decisions.
4. **Historical cohort, not survivors.** Reuse only a documented point-in-time cohort and causal listing-age/trailing-liquidity guard. Retain subsequent losers and predeclare symbol identity/delisting treatment. A fixed 2019 cohort does not establish a universal evolving-market result. Do not substitute a later winner or use full-test liquidity to admit assets.
5. **Fail closed, locally and honestly.** Missing entry quote means skip/log; missing required held mark/exit or uncertain identity makes the affected candidate-period incomplete. No executable forward fill, future last-bar liquidation or silent removal of bad days. Do not call an uncomputed candidate a losing strategy, and do not let an unrelated asset error masquerade as a gap in an otherwise clean period.

### H1: daily reversal with hourly execution

BTC/ETH only. For completed daily log return `r_t`, require all three: `r_t < 0`; `(r_t - mean(previous 30 daily log returns excluding current)) / sampleSD(previous 30 excluding current) < -1`; and `v_t = log(Q_t / arithmeticMean(previous 30 daily quote volumes excluding current)) < 0`. The one-sigma threshold is a preregistered technical definition, not a paper-derived estimate. At least 32 daily warmup closes are needed for the previous-return baseline.

Execution is **one full hourly bar after the daily feature close**, daily at 01:00 UTC; the neighbor waits two full hours, daily at 02:00. This explicitly replaces the initial proposal's generic whole-day requirement for H1 only. Verify timestamp-complete hourly execution coverage before financial results. Do not describe this as an hourly-shock rule, a five-minute paper replication or true quoted-liquidity provision. Daily net-delta rebalancing supplies the holding/exit rule; unchanged target exposure is not forcibly roundtripped. Base allocation is 0.5 per triggered eligible asset, unused sleeves cash.

H1 development is 2022 and stress is January–November 2024, chosen from timestamp coverage only before outputs. The large-cap BTC/ETH restriction is a weak-prior adaptation, not proof the published effect exists there. A full-hour delay remains a modeled historical candle-open availability assumption, not an observed bid/ask fill guarantee.

### H2: relative low-activity selection

Use the seven-asset historical 2019 cohort and unchanged causal eligibility. No return, trade-count, taker-share or Amihud filter is added. Daily 00:00 UTC net-delta rebalancing uses the completed daily feature at index `i-2` for a full one-day delay; neighbor uses `i-3`, a full two-day delay. Periods are development 2020–2022, validation 2023 and stress January–November 2024, all previously observed.

### Common accounting and comparisons

Portfolio risk scaling is `min(1, 0.20 / (sampleSD(last 30 closed daily log portfolio returns at current base weights, ddof=1) * sqrt(365)))`, applied to base weights only; the risk window may include the current completed feature return. Zero/invalid standard deviation means cash. Each segment starts flat with 1,000 USDT; no leverage/shorting, target weights at most one, sell-before-buy cash/fee reserve and initial/rebalance/terminal costs apply. Daily liquidation-value NAV is used.

Per-side fee/slippage/full-spread basis points are base `10/1/2`, adverse `15/5/10`, severe `20/10/20`; charge half full spread on each side. Compare historically eligible EW at the same cadence/lag and portfolio volatility cap, uncapped EW, BTC/ETH buy-and-hold and cash. H2 requested-target-exposure matched EW is its allocation contrast; H1 matching inherits the timing signal and is diagnostic only. H3 receives no backtest because adequate queue/fill evidence is absent.
